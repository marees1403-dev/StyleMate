## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a whimsical, affectionate, and tactile shopping universe inspired by doll-scale charm, soft toy textures, and contemporary pastel streetwear. Built for families, young trendsetters, and Gen Z aesthetic shoppers browsing for Men, Women, and Kids styles, the experience balances toy-like joy with polished commercial utility.

The aesthetic fuses **Soft Tactile Playfulness** with **Modern Clean Mobile E-Commerce**:
- **Plump & Cushioned UI**: Surfaces, buttons, and badges carry soft pill shapes and puffy dimensionality reminiscent of stitched garments and cloud-like foam padding.
- **Warm Dreamscape Palette**: Gentle lavender, blushing rose, warm vanilla cream, and soft lilac form a cozy base, avoiding sterile digital whites.
- **Warm Human Contrast**: Text and icons leverage deep aubergine-charcoal rather than harsh pure black, preserving warmth while securing full WCAG AA legibility across small handheld screens.

## Colors

The palette directly references the chibi plush aesthetic, using balanced confectionery pastels alongside accessible dark ink tones.

- **Primary (`#9D85C5`)**: Sweet Wisteria Lavender. Used for key call-to-actions, active navigation markers, selection rings, and core interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#F7BAC5`)**: Petal Blush Pink. Used for favorites, romantic tags, promotional highlights, badge pips, and playful accent cards.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DEF8`)**: Soft Cloud Lilac. Deployed as interactive hover/pressed states, soft chip surfaces, and secondary button backgrounds.
- **Neutral Base (`#2D253A`)**: Deep Aubergine Charcoal. Replaces standard `#000000` to deliver crisp typographic hierarchy, deep borders, and accessible clarity without cold severity.
- **Surface Foundations**:
  - App Canvas: Cream Vanilla (`#FFFDF9`)
  - Elevated Cards & Sheets: Crisp Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle Cream Bordering (`#F8F4EE`)
  - Inset Fields & Muted Dividers: Lavender Pearl (`#F3EEF9`)

## Typography

The type scale combines the rounded, buoyant geometry of **Quicksand** for headlines and expressive promotional banners with the clean readability and balanced horizontal rhythm of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy, micro-interactions, prices, and catalog metadata.

- **Headlines (Quicksand)**: Set generously with rounded terminals. Used in product collection names, promotional greetings, AI outfit suggestions, and dialog headers.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures instant scanability of product descriptions, size guides, checkout details, and filter chips. 
- **Prices & Currency**: Always set in `label-lg` with `700` weight to stand out playfully within the pastel canvas.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid touch interaction on mobile screens, utilizing a responsive 4-column system on phones that shifts to 8 and 12 columns on tablets and wider viewports.

- **Safe Zones & Margins**: Canvas edges enforce a consistent `1.25rem` (20px) margin, creating an airy frame around content cards.
- **Product Card Grids**: Dual-column feeds use a `1rem` (16px) gutter, providing ample space for floating badge overlays and heart icon taps without accidental touches.
- **Horizontal Carousels**: Story cards, recently viewed items, and "Style with Family" bundles flow across the screen with right padding bleed to invite horizontal thumb swipes.
- **Docked Thumb Navigation**: Primary cart checkout and segment selectors sit docked along the bottom safe area with `space-md` internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through **puffy, colored ambient shadows** and **cushioned pill layers**, avoiding sharp grayscale drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Vanilla cream ground (`#FFFDF9`) used as the resting base.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf)**: White surfaces elevated by an ultra-soft tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 18px rgba(157, 133, 197, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action & Segment Bars)**: Pill tabs and primary buy buttons: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(157, 133, 197, 0.22)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & AI Floating Assist)**: Docked styling assistants and quick-shop drawers: `box-shadow: 0 -8px 32px rgba(45, 37, 58, 0.08)`.
- **Subtle Inner Glow**: Active pill selectors feature an inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` for a glossy, marshmallow-like tactile quality.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-soft, rounded physical language (`roundedness: 3`):

- **Pill Geometry (9999px / `rounded-full`)**: Applied to all category switchers (Men / Women / Kids), size selectors, action buttons, price badges, and bottom navigation pill containers.
- **Plush Cards (24px / 1.5rem)**: Product listing cards, outfit moodboards, and promotional highlight banners feature deep 24px corner radii.
- **Inner Controls (16px / 1rem)**: Text inputs, quantity steppers, and thumbnail wrappers use softened 16px corners to remain cohesive with the overall silhouette.

## Components

### Category Segment Controller (Women / Men / Kids)
- Fixed or sticky pill-bar container floating above the product feed with a soft Lilac tint (`#F3EEF9`).
- Active segment glides with a spring animation, filling with Lavender (`#9D85C5`), pure white text, and a puffy shadow.
- Inactive segments feature Charcoal text (`#2D253A`) with subtle 60% opacity.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action ("Add to Bag", "Style Me")**: Fully rounded pill, saturated Lavender (`#9D85C5`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` vertical padding, with a subtle bouncy scale down (`transform: scale(0.97)`) on press.
- **Secondary Action ("Try AI Match", "Wishlist")**: Blush Pink background (`#FFDDE2`) with Charcoal text (`#2D253A`) or sheer border outline.
- **Floating Heart / Like Button**: Pure white circle (`44px × 44px`) with soft peach/pink drop shadow and an animated spring heart icon.

### Product & Moodboard Cards
- White background with `24px` rounded corners and faint border (`1px solid #F8F4EE`).
- Top-right floating tag with pill-radius for discounts or new arrivals (e.g., `#F7BAC5` petal pink chip with white text).
- Rounded thumbnail container (`20px`) with a soft background gradient tint (`#F8F4EE` to `#FFFDF9`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Full pill radius search bar with `#F3EEF9` fill, no harsh outer strokes.
- Lavender placeholder text with an inset sparkle AI icon on the trailing edge.

### Chips & Badges
- Miniature pill badges (`11px - 12px` font) used for color swatches, size indicators, and eco/fabric tags.
- Selected state toggles to solid Lavender or Blush Pink with bold contrast.

### AI StyleMate Floating Assistant
- Floating docked widget at the bottom right rendered with a gradient from Petal Pink to Soft Lavender.
- Features a glowing aura shadow and miniature avatar icon that triggers personalized family or capsule wardrobe recommendations.
